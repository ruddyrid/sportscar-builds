# Images — this vehicle's photos

Photos for this build live in this folder, next to the page that
uses them. Nothing is hot-linked from elsewhere, so the entry stays
self-contained if it is copied out on its own.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.webp` — for example
`2026-11-08-brake-caliper-before.webp`. Date first keeps the folder
in chronological order. One subject per filename, lowercase, hyphens
only, no spaces.

## Format and size

- **WebP preferred**, JPG as fallback for older tools. Export at
  **1600px on the long edge maximum**, quality ~80 — that keeps most
  photos under 300KB without visible loss.
- Keep the original full-size photo out of the repo (local backup
  only). The repo holds the web-sized copy.
- If a photo shows a part number, measurement, or label the text
  relies on, say so in the caption — do not make the reader zoom to
  find it.

## Captions

Every photo on the page gets a caption with a date and one line of
context: what it shows and why it matters. A photo without a caption
is decoration; this library is not a gallery site.

## What not to upload

- Number plates, addresses, or anything identifying a person who
  did not agree to be in the build record.
- Screenshots of someone else's copyrighted material (manual pages,
  forum posts). Describe or paraphrase instead.
