How this library works

1. One car, one folder

Each build lives in its own folder with its page, build log, parts list, changelog, and photos. Copy a folder out and it still works on its own.

2. Specs are verified, not invented

Factory figures come first. Anything from a video, forum, or memory is labeled as stated and unverified until it is checked against the factory service manual.

3. Follow it your way

New builds and major updates go into the RSS feed — subscribe in any feed reader. There is no account to make and no platform in the middle.

4. Same structure, every car

Every entry follows the same template: spec sheet, build story, parts list, gallery, changelog. Learn one page and you can read them all. See the user guide.

A note on specifications

Specifications vary by year, trim, market, and part manufacturer. Treat the numbers on these pages as a starting point for inspection, and always confirm the final specification in the factory service manual or the documentation for the exact parts you are using.
