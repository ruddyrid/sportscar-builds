# Build Log — 1987 Mazda RX-7 Turbo II

One dated entry per work session, newest first. The video records
further down have no recorded dates: they stay in the order the source
material presented them, and no dates are inferred. Compiled from the
owner's @ruddyrid videos and TealsUnltd tech posts — method records,
not transcripts. Torque figures are recorded as stated in the video
and flagged for verification; they are not converted or smoothed.

## 2026-11-11 — Phase 2: spec sheet verified against the FSM review

- **Did:** Applied the owner's 13BT accuracy review (2026-11-08),
  drawing on its 21 ERROR corrections, to this entry's spec sheet.
  Verified against its factory service manual (FSM) citations, not
  against memory.
- **Corrected:** power from "185 hp @ 6,500 — verify" to **182 hp
  (136 kW) @ 6,500 rpm, US SAE net**. The 185 figure is the JDM
  185 PS rating; it is now labeled as such, not mixed with hp.
- **Added (verified):** compression 8.5:1 (S4 turbo); redline 7,000
  rpm; port injection, 4 injectors (550 cc each); transmission noted
  as manual-only.
- **Kept flagged:** boost. Published manifold readings (~5.5 psi) and
  the 1988 FSM Technical Data figure (45.2 kPa / 6.56 psi) differ by
  about 1 psi and the measurement point could not be confirmed — the
  row stays labeled "as stated, unverified" rather than smoothed.
- **Left out:** fuel-cut rpm, curb weight, and dimensions. No
  FSM-verified figure for them is established in the review, so they
  are absent and flagged here instead of guessed.
- **Verified:** the turbo-to-manifold torque stated in the video
  (37 ft-lb, or "410 inch pounds") sits inside the 1988 FSM range of
  32–40 ft-lb / 44–54 Nm. Note the video's own two numbers disagree
  (410 in-lb ≈ 34.2 ft-lb); both stay recorded as stated, and the FSM
  range is the reference.

### Turbo Manifold Stoning and Install (Series 4 block, Series 5 turbo)

- **Did:** Stoned (resurfaced with a sharpening stone) the turbo
  manifold faces, chose gaskets and copper-plated hardware, then
  installed the manifold.
- **Found:** Turbo-to-manifold torque stated in the video as "410
  inch pounds or 37 foot pounds" — recorded as stated. Verified
  2026-11-11 against the 1988 FSM turbo-to-manifold range,
  32–40 ft-lb (44–54 Nm): 37 ft-lb sits inside it, but the video's
  paired 410 in-lb is about 34.2 ft-lb, so the two stated numbers
  disagree. Both stay as stated; use the FSM range.
- **Parts used:** See parts list.
- **Photos:** to add.

### Freeze Plug Install and Coolant Bypass (20 mm, 13B)

- **Did:** Plugged the 13B rotor housing coolant ports that fed
  the lower intake manifold, to reduce intake heat and avoid
  coolant spills on manifold removal.
- **Parts used:** Freeze Plug Set — Rotor Housing Coolant Passage
  (Pair) — the same product shown in the video.
- **Photos:** to add.

### Custom Turbo Coolant Pipe (FC3S)

- **Did:** Replaced the turbo water feed with a stainless hard line
  fed from the rear iron. The job included a brass-solder joint
  problem and a tight final clearance.
- **Photos:** to add.

### Downpipe Install (Racing Beat 3-inch, FC3S)

- **Did:** Installed the Racing Beat downpipe; a modified wrench
  was needed for the lower turbo-outlet bolt.
- **Found:** A small exhaust-leak area was noted in the video
  description — whether it was later fixed is unknown; flagged.
- **Photos:** to add.

### Intake Manifold and Turbo Install, PTFE Gaskets

- **Did:** Installed the intake manifold and turbo with Teals
  Durlon 9400 carbon-filled PTFE (polytetrafluoroethylene) gaskets
  (S4 Turbo II thermal lower intake manifold gasket, replaces
  N318-13-111D).
- **Found:** Gaskets proven on the car multiple times; latest run
  about 1,200 miles without issues. They seal dry, with no RTV
  (room-temperature vulcanizing sealant).
