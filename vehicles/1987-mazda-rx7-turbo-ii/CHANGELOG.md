### Added

- Verified spec rows: compression 8.5:1 (S4 turbo), 7,000 rpm
  redline, port injection with 4 × 550 cc injectors, transmission
  noted as Turbo II manual-only.

  ~5.5 psi manifold readings versus the 1988 FSM Technical Data

- Build-log entry for the 2026-11-11 verification pass.

### Changed

- Accuracy-review reference now names the owner's 13BT review
  (2026-11-08) and treats its 21 ERROR corrections as the source of
  the verified figures here.

### Fixed

- Spec-sheet power corrected from 185 hp to **182 hp (136 kW) @
  6,500 rpm, US SAE net**. The 185 PS JDM rating is now labeled as
  JDM instead of being presented as US hp.
